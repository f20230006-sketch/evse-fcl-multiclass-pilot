# Experiment protocol

Preserved configuration records: configs/multiclass_full_comparison.json, replay_followup_essential.json, replay_followup_sweep.json, balanced_rare_final.json, targeted_validation_2026_10_27.json, and cbrs_comparison_2026_10_28.json. The four cohorts are reconnaissance (R), flooding (F), low-rate rare (L), and vulnerability (V). The configs specify the three orders. Five heterogeneous constructed profiles use persistent fitting/calibration/evaluation roles; preprocessing is fitted on Task 1 fitting data only. Training uses five FedAvg rounds per task and five local epochs per round. The focal memory budgets are 60 and 300 examples per client, with pooled controls matching the total budget. The ten training seeds are 42–51; role split 42 is primary, and splits 43–45 repeat RBR versus class-balanced at 60. CBRS was evaluated on split 42 only.

Implemented comparisons include fine-tuning, uniform, protected, class-balanced, CBRS (population rule under common retrieval), EWC, A-GEM, joint, pooled training, and rare+random ablation. No DER++ results exist. Balanced fill can keep additional rare rows beyond the reservation; the allocator redistributes unused capacity and stores unique row IDs.

Merged-Scan evaluation sums six scan probabilities before argmax; original task cohorts and separate Slowloris labels remain. Exact-type macro-F1 uses the original taxonomy. Split comparisons average orders within seed before calculating paired unadjusted t intervals. Joint training is an all-data reference, not a guaranteed ceiling. The five profiles are constructed; orders also change the data used to fit preprocessing.

A read-only rescore excluded 9–16 evaluation rows per split/order that matched fitting vectors or fitting/calibration source fields. Federated RBR retention, Slowloris and forgetting contrasts with class-balanced across all splits and CBRS on split 42 retained their nominal significance. Worst-client gains over CBRS on split 42 and class-balanced on split 44 did not. Exact-type federated macro-F1 comparisons averaged over orders still included zero. This audit reused checkpoints and does not demonstrate independence. On split 42, 57/59 source files contribute to both fitting and evaluation. No hardware deployment was verified.

For context, see McMahan et al., AISTATS 2017 (FedAvg); Chrysakis and Moens, ICML 2020 (CBRS); Kirkpatrick et al., PNAS 2017 (EWC); and Chaudhry et al., ICLR 2019 (A-GEM).
