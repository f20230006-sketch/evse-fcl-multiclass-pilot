# CBRS comparison: design fixed before fitting

Use Algorithm 1 of Chrysakis and Moens, ICML 2020, including its persistent full-class status, not a fixed class-quota sampler. Source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/chrysakis20a/chrysakis20a.pdf ; supplementary proof: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/chrysakis20a/chrysakis20a-supp.pdf . Full status begins only in a filled memory, consistent with supplementary Lemma A.1. All tied largest classes become full. A non-full incoming class replaces a uniformly chosen stored member of any tied largest class. A full class replaces one of its own members with probability stored-count / encountered-count, with the new encounter included in the denominator. Full flags and encounter counters persist; counters do not reset at tasks.

Implement published CBRS population, not the paper's separate weighted-retrieval or changing-loss-weight algorithms.
